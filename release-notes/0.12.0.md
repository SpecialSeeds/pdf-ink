## Key features
- Recolour a selection: the selection bar now has a row of colours. When the selection includes highlighter ink, a second row beneath it recolours just the highlighting, so ink and highlights can be changed separately. One undo reverts it.

## Bugs fixed
- Writing could freeze until Obsidian was sent to the background, when a resting palm interrupted or set off a two-finger zoom. The pen now always comes back, at the latest on its next touch.
- Duplicating a selection near the edge of a page could put the copy off the page, where it couldn't be found. Copies now always land on the page.

## QoL improvements
- Recolouring a selection keeps every item selected, including ones that were already the chosen colour.
