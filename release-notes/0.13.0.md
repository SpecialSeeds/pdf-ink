## Key features
- Scroll lock: a new button beside the fit buttons locks scrolling to up and down only, or left and right only. With left and right locked, a swipe that wanders sideways still only scrolls down the page. It works with fingers, the trackpad and the scroll wheel, and is remembered across tabs.
- Fit height replaces Fit page. It fills the pane with the page's height and lets you pan across, which on a landscape page is the whole page in view. Tabs saved at Fit page reopen at Fit height.

## Bugs fixed
- Undo, redo, switching tools and zooming could lag, most of all in long notebooks and on large pages and boards.

## QoL improvements
- Undo and redo now redraw only the page that changed, instead of every page in view twice over.
- Zooming no longer redraws a large page's ink more than once.
- Saving no longer causes a pause a couple of seconds after you stop writing.
- Fewer commands. These were removed because the toolbar, the tool palette, gestures, shortcuts or settings already cover them: Search notes, Toggle paper tint, Toggle light or dark pages, Undo stroke, Redo stroke, Toggle eraser mode, Toggle eraser, Toggle selection tool, Insert page below, Insert page above, Page options, Toggle zoom lock and Move tool palette to the other side. If you had a hotkey bound to one of them, it no longer does anything.
