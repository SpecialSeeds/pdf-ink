## Key features
- Find in PDF: search a PDF's text with the magnifier in the top bar, Ctrl/Cmd+F, or the command. Every match is highlighted, the first one at or after your page is shown straight away, and Enter, Shift+Enter or the arrows step through the rest. Case, accents and line breaks do not get in the way. Highlights are never saved or exported.
- PDF links work: tap one with a finger (once you have used a pen) or Ctrl/Cmd+click it. A link within the document jumps to its place and offers "Back to page N" to return; web and email links open in your browser. The pen still draws over links.
- Two-finger tap window: a new setting for how long a two-finger tap may take, from 300 to 2000 ms. Raise it if your taps are missed.

## Bugs fixed
- A palm resting on the page while writing no longer switches to the eraser when it lifts.
- Zooming no longer loses your place. Deep into a long PDF it could land more than a page away from where you were.
