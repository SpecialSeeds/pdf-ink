## Key features
- Insert images: choose a picture with the new button on the ink palette or the "Insert image" command, or paste or drop one onto a page. It is saved as an attachment, lands selected so you can move, resize or rotate it at once, and is included when you export. Erasers leave pictures alone; select one and press Delete to remove it.
- Move a selection to another page: drag a lasso selection onto any other page and it lands there under your pen. Hold it near the top or bottom of the view to scroll to a page further away. One undo puts it back.
- Reading mode: the book button in the top bar, or the "Toggle reading mode" command, shows your ink without the palette. Nothing draws, the pen and fingers scroll, and a tap follows a link. Each tab remembers it.
- Dashed and dotted shapes: pick Solid, Dashed or Dotted in the Shapes menu. With shapes selected, choosing a style changes them too.

**Update on every device.** Annotations saved by this version use a new file format so pictures are never lost, and earlier versions of the plugin will not open them.
